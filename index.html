Stoma Daily Review

7-Day Stoma Daily Review Checklist

The seven review days are calculated automatically from the Surgery Date, starting on the 1st post-operative day. The surgery date itself is Day 0 / operation day and is not counted as a review day. Any post-operative review days before the selected review date are greyed and crossed out as missed.

Day 1 starts the day after surgery.
