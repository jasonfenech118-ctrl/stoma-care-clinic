Add Quick Look to your Home Screen
On Android — Chrome
  1. Tap the menu ⋮ at the top-right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Tap Add / Install — a Quick Look button appears on your home screen.
On iPhone / iPad — Safari
  1. Tap the Share button ↑ at the bottom.
  2. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add — a Quick Look button appears on your home screen.
On this device
  1. Open your browser's menu.
  2. Choose Install or Add to Home screen.
